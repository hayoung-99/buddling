Your computer will warn you

This app is not code signed. Signing means paying Apple and Microsoft every year for a certificate, and we are not there yet. So your operating system tells you it cannot verify who made it.

That does not mean the app is dangerous, but it does mean nobody is vouching for it. Which is why the source code is fully public. The steps below get you past the warning once.

macOS Unidentified developer

  1. Move the app into your Applications folder.
  2. Right click the app and choose Open. Right click, not double click.
  3. Press Open in the dialog once. It opens normally from then on.
If it still says the app is damaged, run this once in Terminal.
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/tap-tap.app

Windows SmartScreen

  1. Double click the installer you downloaded.
  2. When the blue Windows protected your PC box appears, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway and the install continues.
It never asks for administrator rights. It installs for your account only and can be removed from the usual apps list.

Questions

Things you might wonder

What is tap-tap?

A macOS and Windows app that keeps a 3D animal character on your desktop. Tap your own character and everyone on that team sees theirs dance. It is free and there is no account.

Do my teammates need it too?

Yes. The character has to be on their desktop for the nudge to land. One person creates a team, gets an invite code, and everyone else types it in.

What does an invite code look like?

Six characters, letters and numbers, with the easily confused 0, O, 1 and I left out. It expires 24 hours after it is issued and you can draw a new one any time.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid tier. The source is public, so you can build it yourself instead if you prefer.

Can I use it alone?

A character appears for each team you are in, so you need at least one team. A team of one is still a team, and the character lives on your desktop just the same. There is simply nobody to nudge yet.

Can I install it on a work computer?

The Windows installer never asks for administrator rights and installs for your account only. It is not code signed though, so company policy may still stop it. If somebody needs to check, point them at the GitHub repository.

Will it get in the way?

Clicks in the empty space around the character pass straight through to your desktop, so your icons stay reachable. It makes no sound, and the menu bar icon hides every character at once.

Why does it say "unidentified developer"?

Because it is not code signed yet, and that certificate costs money every year. It does not mean the app is dangerous, it means nobody is vouching for it. On macOS right click and choose Open; on Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. The steps are just above.
